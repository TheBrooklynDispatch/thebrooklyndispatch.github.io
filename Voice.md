# The Brooklyn Dispatch Voice

> Write like a person whose recommendations matter because they are earned.

---

# The Goal

Readers should finish an article thinking:

"I trust this person's taste."

Not:

"This person is trying to impress me."

---

# The Voice

The writing should be:

Curious.

Accessible.

Understated.

Conversational.

Opinionated without becoming dismissive.

Confident without becoming certain.

Personal without becoming self-indulgent.

---

# The Reader

Assume the reader is intelligent.

Never write down to them.

Never write to prove how much you know.

Invite them into the conversation.

---

# Style

Prefer clarity over cleverness.

Short sentences are welcome.

Long sentences are welcome when they reward the reader.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Avoid inflated language.

Write naturally.

---

# Criticism

Criticism exists to deepen appreciation.

Not to deliver verdicts.

A review should leave readers wanting to experience the work for themselves.

Whenever possible, explain *why* something works instead of simply saying that it does.

---

# Recommendations

Recommend generously.

Whenever possible, leave readers with somewhere worth going next.

A film.

A book.

A place.

A conversation.

An essay.

---

# Humility

Avoid telling readers what they should think.

Avoid pretending there is only one correct interpretation.

Leave room for disagreement.

Changing your mind is allowed.

---

# AI

Never sound like an AI summarising culture.

Avoid clichés.

Avoid listicle language.

Avoid exaggerated certainty.

Write from experience whenever possible.

Use details that could only come from someone who has genuinely spent time with the work.

---

# Things We Avoid

Clickbait.

Outrage.

Hot takes.

Rage-bait.

Celebrity gossip.

Publishing for algorithms.

Pretending certainty.

Writing to impress.

---

# Before Publishing

Ask:

Would I send this to a close friend?

Would I stand by this in five years?

Does this help someone discover something worthwhile?

Does this sound like The Brooklyn Dispatch?
