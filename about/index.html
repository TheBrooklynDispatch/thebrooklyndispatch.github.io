About

The Brooklyn Dispatch is an independent publication about international and independent cinema, books and New York.

It is interested in criticism over hype, curiosity over certainty, and work that rewards attention.

Film sits at the center: reviews, festival dispatches, essays and conversations. Brooklyn provides the point of view, not a boundary.

For press, submissions or general correspondence: hello@thebrooklyndispatch.com.
