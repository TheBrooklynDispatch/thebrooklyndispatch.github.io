### *The Invite* (2026)

A sharp script and uniformly excellent performances make *The Invite* one of the year's most enjoyable trips to the cinema. It balances tension and humour with impressive confidence, trusting its audience more often than it explains itself.

### *Nina Simone's Gum* (2021)

A surprisingly moving meditation on collecting. Ellis uses a small, almost absurd object to explore memory, grief and the strange ways physical possessions gather emotional weight over time. It has me thinking about why we keep things at all.

### *An Insatiable High* (1977)

A blind record-store purchase that quickly became the soundtrack to my summer. Effortlessly joyful without ever becoming background music, it's the sort of record that rewards both attentive listening and a room full of friends.

### Warsaw, Greenpoint

Stretching the limits of its 1,000-person capacity, Warsaw somehow hosted an Olivia Rodrigo pop-up show this month. The contrast between one of the world's biggest artists and one of New York's smallest venues made the evening feel almost surreal.
