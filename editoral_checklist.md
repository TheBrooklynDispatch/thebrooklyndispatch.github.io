# Editorial Checklist

Before publishing, every piece should pass the following checks.

---

## Purpose

- [ ] Why this?
- [ ] Why now?
- [ ] Is this saying something new or saying something familiar in a better way?

---

## Writing

- [ ] Does the opening paragraph make me want to continue?
- [ ] Have I removed unnecessary words?
- [ ] Does every section earn its place?
- [ ] Have I avoided clichés?
- [ ] Would I enjoy reading this in five years?

---

## Editorial

- [ ] Have I connected this piece to something larger?
- [ ] Is "Where Next" complete?
- [ ] Are the recommendations genuinely additive?
- [ ] Does this fit the voice of The Brooklyn Dispatch?

---

## Presentation

- [ ] Featured artwork selected
- [ ] Pull quotes reviewed
- [ ] Metadata complete
- [ ] Mobile layout checked
- [ ] Reading Mode tested
- [ ] Print layout checked

---

## Final Question

Would I be proud if this were the first article someone ever read on The Brooklyn Dispatch?

If the answer isn't an immediate "yes," keep editing.
