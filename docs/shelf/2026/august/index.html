On the Shelf

Four recommendations from the past month. One film, one book, one album, and one place. Not the best of August, simply four things I think are worth your time.

Film

The Invite (2026)

Olivia Wilde · Director

A sharp script and uniformly excellent performances make The Invite one of the year's most enjoyable trips to the cinema. It balances tension and humour with impressive confidence, trusting its audience more often than it explains itself.

Book

Nina Simone's Gum (2021)

Warren Ellis · Author

A surprisingly moving meditation on collecting. Ellis uses a small, almost absurd object to explore memory, grief, and the strange ways possessions accumulate emotional weight over time. It left me thinking about why we keep things at all.

Album

An Insatiable High (1977)

Masayoshi Takanaka · Artist

A blind record-store purchase that became the soundtrack to my summer. Effortlessly joyful without ever becoming background music, it's the sort of record that rewards both attentive listening and a room full of friends.

Place

Warsaw

Greenpoint · Brooklyn

Stretching the limits of its 1,000-person capacity, Warsaw somehow hosted an Olivia Rodrigo pop-up show this month. The contrast between one of the world's biggest artists and one of New York's smallest venues made the evening feel almost surreal.
