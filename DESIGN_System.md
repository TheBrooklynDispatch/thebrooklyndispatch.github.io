# The Brooklyn Dispatch Design System

> Good design should disappear. The writing should not.

---

# Philosophy

The Brooklyn Dispatch is designed to be read, not browsed.

Every design decision should help readers slow down, pay attention and enjoy spending time with an article.

Restraint is a feature.

---

# Design Principles

## Typography first.

Typography is the primary design element.

Headlines should feel literary.

Body text should feel effortless to read.

Navigation should never compete with the writing.

---

## White space is part of the design.

Crowded pages feel disposable.

Generous spacing encourages slower reading.

---

## Images earn their place.

Every image should contribute to the article.

Avoid decorative filler.

Prefer:

- film posters
- production stills
- festival photography
- original photography

---

## Motion should be subtle.

Animations should support orientation.

Never distract.

---

## Fast feels premium.

Pages should load instantly.

Readers should never wait.

---

# Visual Identity

## Colour Palette

Background

Warm paper

Primary text

Near black

Secondary text

Muted grey

Accent

Lime

The accent colour should be used sparingly.

It should feel like highlighting a sentence in a book.

---

# Typography

Headlines

Elegant serif

Body

Readable serif

Navigation

Simple sans-serif

Metadata

Small uppercase sans-serif

---

# Layout

Maximum reading width:

~700–750px

Homepage:

Wide editorial layout

Articles:

Narrow reading column

Always prioritise readability over filling the screen.

---

# Navigation

Simple.

Film

Books

New York

Notebook

About

Nothing more until we need it.

---

# Homepage

The homepage is the front window.

It should feature one outstanding story.

Everything else supports it.

Avoid information overload.

---

# Articles

Every article should contain:

Title

Dek

Hero image

Reading time

Publication date

Body

Where Next

Related reading

Comments

---

# Reviews

Reviews are essays.

Avoid scores.

Avoid stars.

Conclusions should explain why the work matters.

---

# Photography

Natural.

Quiet.

Editorial.

Avoid stock photography.

---

# Accessibility

Readable font sizes.

Excellent contrast.

Keyboard friendly.

Fast on mobile.

---

# The Feeling

The Brooklyn Dispatch should feel like:

An independent bookstore.

A quiet cinema before the lights dim.

A Sunday morning with nowhere else to be.
